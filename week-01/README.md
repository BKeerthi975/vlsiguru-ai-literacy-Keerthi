## Reflection

### What I understood

Not everything that looks smart is AI. An LLM builds its answer one token at a time by predicting what comes next, so a fluent answer is not proof of a correct one. Agents are systems built around a model, not just a better chatbot.

### What still confuses me

I understand next-token prediction at an intuitive level, but not how models are trained further to follow instructions, or how they decide between sampling and picking the top token.

### One thing I will verify differently next time

I will open every link and check every citation myself before using it, instead of trusting a source an AI suggested.
